    Health and allergy disclaimer

    NutriPlan is for general information and education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and using it does not create a doctor–patient or dietitian–client relationship. Calories, weights and meal ideas are estimates based on BMI and the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, and they may not suit your body.

    Please talk to a qualified doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition (for example diabetes, heart, kidney or thyroid problems, or an eating disorder), or take medication. Stop and get medical help if you feel unwell.

    The food suggestions are not allergen-safe. Ingredients, brands and cross-contamination vary, so always read food labels and ask about ingredients. If you have a serious allergy, check with your doctor first.

    Privacy

    This page has no accounts, ads, analytics or cookies, and it does not send your details anywhere. What you enter (age, sex, height, weight, activity, food preference, allergies, goal weight and foods you log) is processed in your browser and saved only on your own device, using local storage, so the page can remember you. The website host may keep standard technical logs.

    The Share and Download PDF buttons create your meal plan on your device. Sharing sends only the meal plan text (no age, weight or allergies) to the app or person you choose.

    The Useful tools section may contain affiliate links to other websites, such as Amazon. If you click one, that website has its own privacy policy and may use cookies to record that you came from NutriPlan. NutriPlan does not send them anything you entered.

    You can remove your saved data at any time with the button below, or by clearing your browser data.
